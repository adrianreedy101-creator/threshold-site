What The Missing Link actually is

A diagnostic, not a quiz

Validated by the Barrett Values Centre. It maps your personal values hierarchy: what you say drives you versus what actually does.

A starting point, not a solution

The assessment reveals the gap between your stated values and your actual operating system. Threshold is where you close it.

The door, not the room

You don't pay for The Missing Link separately. It's the first step inside Threshold, available to accepted applicants only.
